# TER v3.6 — Advanced Intelligence Architecture

TER v3.6 is a structural evolution of the existing TER platform, not a new isolated application. Existing engines remain in place while a new Intelligence Core connects them through five controls:

- **Provenance**: source, origin, timestamp, quality and dependencies.
- **Confidence**: data quality, model confidence, agreement, freshness and contradiction penalties.
- **Contradiction Engine**: explicit conflicts between technical, macro, fundamental and news evidence.
- **Decision Frame**: conditional probabilities, scenario framing and invalidation conditions.
- **Orchestration**: a pipeline vocabulary from ingestion through audit.

## New endpoint

`GET /api/intelligence/overview` aggregates the current market, signal, macro, news and fundamental engines into a single analytical snapshot.

## New interface

`#/intelligence` exposes the Intelligence Control Plane with control metrics, contradictions, provenance, decision framing and conditional scenarios.

All outputs remain analytical/simulated. Synthetic data is explicitly labelled and no real order execution is introduced by this evolution.

## Intelligence Mesh — évolution structurelle

La plateforme v3.6 ajoute une couche de **Evidence Graph** au-dessus des moteurs existants. Le graphe relie marché, macro, fondamentaux, news, risque, signaux et Decision Frame. Chaque relation est explicitement typée comme `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS` ou `INFORMS` et reçoit une force indicative.

Le Control Plane expose également le **pipeline observable** : `INGEST → NORMALIZE → FEATURES → SIGNALS → ENSEMBLE → CALIBRATION → RISK → SCENARIO → DECISION → AUDIT` avec état, latence indicative et nombre de dépendances.

Cette évolution ne crée pas un nouveau produit séparé : elle approfondit le noyau TER v3.6 et prépare la convergence des moteurs vers une architecture d'intelligence unifiée.
